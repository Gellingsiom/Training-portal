Safety & Hygiene Sign Library

A growing collection of practical signs and reminders for kitchens, washrooms, cleaning stores and hospitality workplaces. Search the library, choose a paper size and print the sign you need.

Important — two different types of signage: These Gellings designs are branded reminder posters and supplementary notices. They are not replacements for prescribed prohibition, warning, mandatory, emergency-exit, first-aid or fire-equipment signs. Those signs need the correct standard pictogram, shape, colours and placement. Isle of Man Government consulted on draft Safety Signs and Signals Regulations in 2025, but its published update on 13 August 2026 says the proposed tranche was paused until further notice. Do not describe these posters as statutory or certified signs. Always follow the workplace risk assessment, fire plan, product label and safety data sheet.
